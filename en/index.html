How we work

How it works

1

You call or email

Tell us in two sentences what needs doing. Photos help, but are not required.

2

We come and survey — no obligation

We measure, look at the structure and think along with you. Often we spot a solution that is cheaper or more practical. Takes 30–60 minutes.

3

A quote and a date

An itemised quote with a start date and a duration. Not "sometime this spring".

4

We build it and hand it over

The same team, the same point of contact, tidied up every day. At handover we walk through it together. Anything not right, we put right.

Why Haaks

Why people call us — and call us back

Small jobs welcome too

We deliberately keep room in the schedule for jobs of a few days. A dormer or a knock-through is not an afterthought here.

Our tradespeople are on our payroll

Bricklayers, carpenters and roofers employed by us. That is how we can schedule what we promise.

One contractor for the whole job

Structural work, tiling, plastering, services — we also arrange the trades we do not do ourselves. You do not have to coordinate anyone.

A fixed price, not hourly billing

For the vast majority of our work we set a fixed contract sum. Only for genuinely unpredictable work do we charge on a time-and-materials basis — and we agree that up front.

A 10-year guarantee on our work

In writing. Call us within that period and we will come round.

Known in the Kampen region

180+ jobs nearby. Chances are we have already worked for someone on your street — feel free to ask for an address.
